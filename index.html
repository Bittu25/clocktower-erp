Clocktower ERP Concept
Concept proposal · not an official BITS Pilani product

Student ERP redesign · Pilani · Goa · Hyderabad · Dubai

Clocktower

One mobile-first app for a BITSian's whole journey, from the BITSAT offer to the alumni transcript request. It replaces the patchwork of ERP, LMS, SWD, PSD and placement portals with one login, and it borrows its interaction patterns from apps students already open fifty times a day.

One login, one inboxEvery portal, notice and request behind a single BITS Google sign-in.
Every request is trackableLeave, bonafide, re-eval and no-dues show who holds it and when it will move, like a food order.
No more racesCourse registration and PS allotment run on submitted preferences, not on who clicks first at 10:00.

01 · Audit of today

A student touches nine systems in one semester

This is the typical setup students describe across campuses. Names vary a little by campus; the pattern does not.

  • HIGHRegistration is a race. Sections fill in minutes, the ERP slows under load, and clashes show up only after you submit.
  • HIGHRequests disappear. A bonafide certificate or leave request has no visible status, so students email and walk to offices to ask.
  • HIGHDesktop-first screens. PeopleSoft menus are nested and hard to use on a phone, where students do almost everything else.
  • MEDNotices are noise. Important dates are lost between club mails, and seniors' WhatsApp forwards become the source of truth.
  • MEDMarks without meaning. Component marks sit in the LMS, grades in the ERP, and nobody shows what you need in the compre.
  • MEDGraduation by signature hunt. No-dues still means walking to the library, hostel, department and accounts.

Today tab

Opens to the answer, not a menu

  • The next class, its room and directions sit on top, so the most common question takes zero taps.
  • "Needs you" holds only items with a deadline or a pending decision. Everything else waits in its tab.
  • Notices are stories with a ring per source. Seen ones turn grey; any club can be muted.
  • During compre weeks the banner switches to your next paper and seat, and club notices go quiet.

Borrowed from Instagram (stories), Google Calendar (next up), WhatsApp (actionable alerts).

Registration

A cart instead of a race

  • The cart is open for weeks. Students plan whenever they want, so there is no load spike at opening minute.
  • Picking a section paints the week grid instantly. A clash turns red before you can submit it. Try switching CS F364 to L2.
  • Submissions in the window are allotted together by the existing priority rules (year, CGPA, core vs elective), then everyone is notified at once.
  • Add/drop happens in the same cart with live seat counts, not a separate form.

Borrowed from Amazon (cart), BookMyShow (seats left), and the lesson of IRCTC Tatkal: don't make people race a server.

Request tracker

Every request shows who has it and when it moves

  • One tracker for leave, certificates, makeup, re-evaluation, complaints and no-dues.
  • Each step names the office or person holding it and the typical time, computed from past requests.
  • A stuck step past its usual time escalates automatically, so students don't have to chase.
  • Finished documents arrive as signed PDFs and go to DigiLocker.

Borrowed from Swiggy / Zomato (live order status), DigiLocker (verified documents).

Wallet

All dues, one list, two taps

  • Fees, mess bills, library fines and event charges collected from every division into one list.
  • Select and pay with UPI. Receipts save automatically; parents can be given view access.
  • Optional autopay for the monthly mess bill.
  • The same tab holds the ID card and gate pass QR, which work offline.

Borrowed from GPay / PhonePe (UPI and ledger), CRED (calm due-date reminders).

Grades

Marks that tell you what to do next

  • Component marks from the LMS and final grades from the ERP finally live in one place.
  • The compre calculator answers the question every student asks in late November. Tap a target grade.
  • A CGPA what-if helps before branch transfer, dual-degree allotment and minor applications.
  • Early warnings go to the student and their advisor, never to a public list.

Borrowed from fitness apps (trend over time) and Spotify Wrapped for the end-of-semester summary.

Practice School

Rank with real information

  • Students rank stations with past cutoffs, seat counts and reviews from seniors who went there.
  • Reordering is direct. On phones, long-press and drag; here, use the arrows.
  • After allotment, the PS diary, mid-term evaluations and the final report all live in the same place.

Borrowed from LinkedIn Jobs and internship apps (role details, reviews).

07 · Everyone else in the loop

Each role sees its own queue

Student flows only get faster if the person on the other side can act in one tap too.

Instructor-in-charge

Publish the handout and evaluation plan once; upload marks per component; set grade cutoffs; approve makeup requests from a queue; message a whole section.

Warden & SWD

Leave and certificate queues with one-tap approve; mess counts per day for kitchens; hostel complaints routed to the right staff with photos; overdue items flagged.

AUGSD / ARC / Timetable

Build the timetable and registration rules, run the preference-based allotment, see demand per course before sections are fixed, publish exam seating.

PSD & Placement Unit

Post stations and companies, run allotment, collect PS evaluations, pull verified student profiles instead of re-collecting CVs and grade sheets.

Parents

Read-only and opt-in by the student: fee dues, leave travel dates, grades only if the student allows. Nothing else.

Alumni

Login stays after graduation for transcripts, degree verification for employers and universities, and the alumni directory.

08 · Design checks

The bar every flow has to clear

These checks were applied to each screen above and should be re-run with students in usability tests each semester.

✓

Thumb reach. Primary actions sit in the bottom half of the screen.

✓

Three-tap rule. The top ten tasks finish in three taps or fewer from Today.

✓

No silent waits. Every request shows status, owner and expected time.

✓

Works on bad Wi-Fi. Timetable, ID card and gate pass are cached offline.

✓

No peak-load races. Preference windows replace first-come slots.

✓

Accessible. WCAG AA contrast in both themes, screen reader labels, large text support.

✓

Calm notifications. Mute per source; quiet hours during midsem and compre.

✓

Student owns privacy. Parent access and grade sharing are the student's choice.

✓

One identity. BITS Google sign-in everywhere; no separate portal passwords.

How to roll it out without replacing the ERP on day one

Phase 1 · Read

Mobile app reads timetable, grades, dues and notices from the current systems through APIs.

Phase 2 · Requests

Leave, certificates, complaints and the tracker move in, with warden and SWD queues.

Phase 3 · Registration

Course cart and preference-based allotment, piloted with one year group first.

Phase 4 · Career & alumni

PS, placements, no-dues automation, DigiLocker and alumni access.
